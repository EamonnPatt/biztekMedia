    04 — FAQ

    Good
    questions.

    What files can I upload?

    Video: MP4, WEBM or MOV up to 250 MB. MP4 with H.264 is the safest choice. If your clip is longer than 6 seconds, you choose which part plays. Images: JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF. Screens are 1920×1080 landscape, and you can also design in 1080×1920 portrait.

    Does the ad type change the price?

    No. Video, image and text ads all cost the same. Every spot is 6 seconds long, and the price depends only on how often it plays, from once every 4 minutes to once a minute. Prices are for 4 weeks at a time, plus HST.

    Where will my ad play?

    On every screen in the gym. Every ad plays on all of them, so there's nothing to choose.

    Do the screens play sound?

    Screens are muted by default. Add “Audio on” to a video ad and it plays with sound on screens that have speakers.

    What won't you run?

    Every ad is reviewed before it airs. We don't run tobacco, vaping, cannabis, alcohol, gambling, adult content, misleading health claims or other gyms. If we can't run your ad, we'll work with you to fix it or refund you.

    Is payment secure?

    Payments go through Helcim's secure checkout. Your card details go straight to Helcim and never touch our servers.
